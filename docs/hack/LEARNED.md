## Jenn
- The state now estimates 91% of the water on city yards is used up rather than flowing back to the rivers, up from an old assumption of 40%. Yards matter more to the Great Salt Lake than we thought.
- The agent's first search said household leaks waste 10,000 gallons a year; opening EPA's live page showed 9,300. The agent finds sources fast, but someone still has to open every link.

## Jennifer
- In a typical gas-heated Utah home, about 60% of the CO2 comes from the electric bill and 40% from the furnace. Utah's grid still put out 1,322 lbs of CO2 per MWh in 2024.
- The agent couldn't open government websites from its sandbox, so it found numbers through search and I opened every link. Sources disagreed more than once (759 vs 774 kWh, $13.14 vs $13.15), and a person had to pick.

## Britney
- About half of a winter inversion comes from vehicles, trains and planes, and lake dust is mostly coarse PM10 that the usual air quality number doesn't fully capture.
- Working with an agent felt like pairing with a fast teammate: it did the typing, and I made the calls and checked the facts.
